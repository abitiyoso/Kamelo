# Stage 4 — Financial receipts, opening refunds and archive review

20 October 2026. Incremental local implementation of the identified finance gaps, retaining the approved backoffice, original financial ledgers and prior applied migrations. Landing v7, Showcase v6, logo, photographs, prices, navigation and membership rules are unchanged.

## Scope and accounting

The existing deposit, supplier-expense and SaaS refund workflows remain the implementation baseline. This stage adds the missing **historical opening-receipt refund** workflow, source-bound receipt documents for resident invoice payments and historical receipts, and non-destructive retention review. It does not retrofit a new accounting system or add an unapproved invoice credit-note/rent-refund policy.

An owner records a refund against one active opening receipt. Cumulative active refunds cannot exceed that receipt, dates cannot precede collection or exceed today, and refund references are unique per receivable even after correction. A refund is a negative OpeningReceipt journal entry on the date money was actually returned. It reduces operating cash and reopens the original debt; it does not cancel the obligation or transfer money. Existing aggregate queries for finance, dashboards, resident balances, reminder eligibility and proof review already sum this journal, so the negative entry propagates without a second cash record. Opening debt remains separate from invoices and deposits.

Corrections are distinct from refunds. A mistaken receipt/refund is reversed exactly on its original date with the original method/reference and opposite delta. Matched entries require the bank match to be revoked first. A receipt with active refunds cannot be reversed until those refunds are corrected. Source row and organization locks plus database guards serialize concurrent refunds. History is append-only. Refund movements support the existing manual bank matching with an OUT direction; receipts remain IN. Matching does not verify a bank account automatically.

## Receipt issuance and access

The owner's Keuangan page offers at most twenty unissued eligible receipt candidates at a time. It previews the actual payer, property, source, gross amount, date, method, reference and retained funds. Issuance requires a fresh digest, confirmation and command key. Exact retries and concurrent different-key requests produce the same single document per Payment or OpeningReceipt. Issue does not record another payment or queue an email.

Documents hold encrypted immutable source snapshots, internal unique receipt numbers and a SHA-256 consistency checksum. A current source projection marks the document active, partially refunded, fully refunded or corrected. Original snapshots remain available for audit; corrected/refunded receipts are not silently rewritten or presented as fresh active cash.

Owner archives and tenant archives paginate twenty documents per page. The tenant's existing Tagihan page includes its receipt archive. Every JSON/view/download request checks a fresh verified account and exact current billing grant. Invoice receipts belong only to that stay. Opening receipts additionally require the existing explicit account/stay/grant share and matching guest/property. Revoking access/share hides those documents from subsequent tenant requests without deleting the owner's archive. Staff cannot issue documents or inspect the owner archive. Internal retention notes/history are owner-only.

Private HTML downloads are self-contained, contain the approved inline logo and escaped text, load no remote resources, and include responsive/print CSS. Browser print can save this document as PDF; a server-generated PDF file or certified signature provider is not added. No script is allowed by the document's Content-Security-Policy. Cache-Control is private/no-store and Referrer-Policy is no-referrer. Downloaded copies can become stale, which the document states. The checksum is not a digital signature; the receipt is not claimed as a tax invoice or a certified electronic signature.

## Retention controls

Issuance starts with KEEP and no invented deletion deadline. Owners can confirm KEEP, HOLD or a dated REVIEW, providing a policy note and current revision. Every change appends a numbered encrypted review record. Owner detail exposes bounded twenty-row audit pages; tenant responses contain no internal policy notes. Stale updates are rejected. Client history navigation retains the original edit revision so browsing audit pages cannot silently bypass concurrent-change checks.

HOLD preserves documents. REVIEW schedules a policy review; it does not authorize destruction. This module has no automatic deletion, erasure endpoint, account closure or invented statutory retention period. Business/legal retention durations, lawful deletion/fulfillment and certified-document requirements need an approved policy before production acceptance. The controls prepare that review while preserving financial evidence. They do not claim legal compliance certification.
