## 3. Product Structure

Maintain two product capability groups.

### Kamelo Core

The main property management application.

Features:
- Dashboard operasional
- Kamar dan penghuni
- Reservasi dan check-in/check-out
- Tagihan bulanan/harian
- Pencatatan pembayaran
- Pengingat pembayaran melalui WhatsApp manual
- Laporan sederhana
- Multi-property management according to subscription limits

### Kamelo Smart

Optional Smart IoT add-on powered by ELENIO technology.

Features:
- Compatible Tuya smart AC controllers
- Smart lighting
- Smart switches
- Smart plugs
- Compatible smart locks
- Device status monitoring
- Room-device mapping
- Supported remote controls

Important:
- Smart IoT is optional.
- Kamelo Core must work independently.
- Never require a Tuya account during initial registration.
- Use mock devices for demonstrations.
- Activate real Tuya functions only through verified and authorized integrations.
- Do not claim unsupported device capabilities.
