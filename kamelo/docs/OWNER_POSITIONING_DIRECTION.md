# 1. STRATEGIC POSITIONING UPDATE

## The Problem with the Previous Positioning

The previous messaging focused too much on generic benefits such as:

- Kelola properti lebih mudah.
- Semua dalam satu aplikasi.
- Operasional lebih cerdas.
- AI-powered property management.

These messages are insufficiently differentiated from existing kos and hotel management applications.

Kamelo must lead with specific owner problems that cost money, consume time, and create operational frustration.

## New Positioning

**Kamelo by ELENIO**

Brand positioning:

"Dari Masalah Properti, Jadi Tindakan Nyata."

Product category:

AI-Assisted Property Operations & Smart Stay.

Primary audience:

- Owners of exclusive boarding houses with approximately 20–60 rooms.
- Owners managing more than one boarding house.
- Guest house operators with approximately 10–30 rooms.
- Small hotel operators with simple operational requirements.

Secondary audience:

- Smaller boarding houses seeking a basic management system.
- Budget hotels interested in Smart Stay.

The product should be perceived as an operational solution for property owners, not merely another room management application.

## Core Commercial Promise

Kamelo helps owners:

1. Know which payments need follow-up.
2. Identify rooms that are empty or not ready for the next guest.
3. Keep tenant complaints and staff tasks from being forgotten.
4. Monitor and control supported room devices.
5. Reduce unnecessary manual check-in work.
6. Stay informed about relevant property and security events.
7. Use LEO AI Agent to prepare and follow up operational actions.

Never promise guaranteed savings or increased revenue without measured evidence.

## Section Headline

PROBLEM → BUSINESS CONSEQUENCE → WHAT KAMELO DOES.

### Pain 1 — Tagihan Belum Masuk

**Headline:**
"Sudah Jatuh Tempo, Tapi Belum Bayar?"

**Problem:**
Owner must manually check payments and remind tenants.

**Consequence:**
Receivables accumulate and cash collection becomes harder to monitor.

**Kamelo Response:**
Track invoice balances, identify overdue accounts, and let LEO prepare payment reminders for owner approval.

### Pain 2 — Kamar Kosong Terlalu Lama

**Headline:**
"Kamar Kosong, Tapi Tidak Ada yang Follow Up?"

**Problem:**
Vacant rooms and booking inquiries are not consistently monitored.

**Consequence:**
Potential rental income is lost while rooms remain vacant.

**Kamelo Response:**
Show vacancy duration, booking inquiries, room readiness, and suggested follow-up tasks.

### Pain 3 — Tagihan Listrik Naik

**Headline:**
"Tamu Sudah Checkout, AC Masih Menyala?"

**Problem:**
AC and lights remain on when a room is no longer being used.

**Consequence:**
Unnecessary electricity consumption may increase operating expenses.

**Kamelo Response:**
Use compatible Smart Room devices and authorized automation to help control non-essential loads after verified vacancy.

Smart devices and installation are optional and sold separately.

Never imply regular software alone can remotely control ordinary AC units without compatible hardware.

### Pain 4 — Komplain Tidak Selesai

**Headline:**
"Penghuni Komplain, Staf Belum Tindak Lanjut?"

**Problem:**
Maintenance issues get lost in WhatsApp conversations or informal instructions.

**Consequence:**
Delayed response and dissatisfied occupants.

**Kamelo Response:**
Provide maintenance tickets, status tracking, staff assignments, and LEO operational summaries.

### Pain 5 — Owner Harus Selalu Memantau

**Headline:**
"Sudah Ada Pengelola, Tapi Masih Harus Cek Semuanya Sendiri?"

**Problem:**
The owner lacks one reliable view of occupancy, collections, staff tasks, and operational incidents.

**Consequence:**
Frequent phone calls, fragmented reports, and repetitive follow-up.

**Kamelo Response:**
Provide a property dashboard and LEO operational briefing based on authorized data.

# 6. HOW KAMELO SOLVES IT

## Section Headline

"Satu Masalah, Ada Cara Menanganinya."

Rather than listing software modules, demonstrate complete workflows.

### Workflow A — Unpaid Rental Invoice

1. Invoice becomes overdue.
2. Kamelo displays the outstanding amount.
3. LEO identifies the required follow-up.
4. LEO prepares the reminder.
5. Owner approves the action.
6. Reminder is sent through an actually configured messaging integration or opened manually through WhatsApp.
7. Payment status updates only after valid payment recording or verification.

### Workflow B — Guest Check-in

1. Guest arrives.
2. Manager confirms check-in.
3. Kamelo generates a secure guest stay link.
4. Guest receives room information and Wi-Fi instructions.
5. Compatible smart lock credentials and Smart Room controls become available if installed and authorized.
6. Guest can access relevant services without installing an application.

### Workflow C — Room Checkout

1. Guest checks out.
2. Kamelo updates booking status.
3. Staff verifies the room is vacant.
4. Approved non-essential devices can be switched off through compatible controllers.
5. Cleaning or inspection tasks are created.
6. Room becomes available when readiness is confirmed.

### Workflow D — Tenant Maintenance Request

1. Tenant reports a problem.
2. Kamelo creates a ticket.
3. Owner/staff is notified within the application.
4. LEO summarizes outstanding work.
5. Staff updates progress.
6. Tenant views the resolution status.

Show each workflow using the actual application UI, not fictional screenshots.

If a workflow is only partially implemented, clearly identify unavailable steps.
